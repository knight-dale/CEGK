About CEGK

Community Empowerment and Growth Kenya (CEGK) is a youth-centered, community-driven organization dedicated to uplifting young people and minors through access to education, skills development, and employment opportunities.

We work closely with communities to identify the challenges affecting youth and develop practical, sustainable solutions that promote self-reliance, dignity, and long-term growth.

Empowerment Integrity Inclusivity Innovation Collaboration Sustainability
Our Foundation
Vision

To build a society where every young person is empowered, educated, and equipped with the skills and opportunities to lead a productive and fulfilling life.

Mission

To empower youth and minors by providing access to quality education, vocational skills, mentorship, and employment opportunities while fostering personal growth and community development.

Our Objectives

What we set out to achieve

Increase access to education for vulnerable youth and minors.

Equip young people with practical skills for employment and entrepreneurship.

Bridge the gap between education and job opportunities.

Provide mentorship and guidance for personal and career growth.

Promote community awareness on youth empowerment and development.

What We Do

Key programs and activities

Program 01

Education Support

  • Scholarships and school supplies for underprivileged children
  • Academic mentorship and tutoring
Program 02

Skills & Vocational Training

  • Training in tailoring, ICT, hairdressing, and carpentry
  • Digital literacy and online job skills
Program 03

Youth Employment & Entrepreneurship

  • Job placement support and career guidance
  • Entrepreneurship training and small business support
Program 04

Mentorship & Personal Development

  • Life skills training — confidence, communication, leadership
  • Peer mentorship and role model engagement
Program 05

Community Outreach & Advocacy

  • Awareness campaigns on education and youth rights
  • Engagement with parents, schools, and local leaders
